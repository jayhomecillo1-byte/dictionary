A dictionary with a quiet Filipino accent.

Talatinigan lets you look up English words, hear them, save the ones worth keeping, and practice them in a quiz.

Look up

Definitions, phonetics, audio, examples and synonyms from the Free Dictionary API.

Keep

Favorites and search history saved in your browser with Local Storage.

Practice

Multiple-choice quizzes built from your saved words, with instant feedback and a final score.

Built with

HTML5, CSS3, vanilla JavaScript, Fetch API with async/await, and GitHub.
